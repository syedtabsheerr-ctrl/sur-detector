01 · Load your Sargam

Paste notes separated by spaces. Example: S R G M P D N S'.
Roman convention: S=Sa, r=komal Re, R=Re, g=komal Ga, G=Ga, M=Ma, m=tivra Ma, P=Pa, d=komal Dha, D=Dha, n=komal Ni, N=Ni. Prefix a dot for lower octave (.S); suffix an apostrophe for upper octave (S'). Hindi swaras are accepted. Use (k) for komal and (t) for tivra.

The score measures pitch closeness, not tone quality, diction, emotion or overall musicianship.
The reference is your notation, not the original MP3. Upload or paste Sargam, choose Sa, and practise one note at a time. The app converts each swara into an equal-tempered pitch target. Use Target tone to hear the note before singing it.
If the microphone is enabled but no pitch appears: check browser microphone permission, choose the correct input in your operating system, move closer to the microphone, sing a steady vowel such as “aa”, and watch the input-level meter. If the input meter is near zero, check microphone volume. Loud background music, harmonics, whispering and breathy notes can confuse pitch detection.
Microphone processing and optional voice recording happen locally in your browser. No audio is uploaded to a server. Microphone access requires HTTPS or localhost.
